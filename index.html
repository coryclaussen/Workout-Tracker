Logging Your Sets

Tap Log Sets under any move. Put the weight and reps for each set. Dumbbells/kettlebells: log one (45 in each hand = 45). Barbell: log the total, bar included (the bar is 45 lbs).

The weight tip averages all your sets. If your last set drops far below the rest, you'll also see a gold note — that weight may be a touch heavy.

Home or Gym Switch

Each day has its own Home / Gym switch. Open a day to set it. On Home, Hammer Strength and other machines are hidden, and any dumbbell tip past your 45 lb dumbbells shows a red flag — use the barbell, a kettlebell, or Rapid Fitness. On Gym, Hammer Strength moves come first, and no flags.

How the Weight Tips Work

First time on a move: it starts from your old weights when it can — from your first 12 weeks, or from a similar lift. Hammer Strength numbers are estimates, so adjust on set one. With no history, it shows a start range.

After that: it averages your sets. Average hits the top reps? Add weight. Mid-range? Hold and build reps. The tip is a guide — your body has the final say.

Backup Your Data

Tap Export to save a backup file of all your logs. Do this once a week, or before a phone update. Lost your data? Tap Import and pick that file to get it all back.

The Game Plan

Weeks 1–4: Build volume. 3 sets of 8–12. Learn the moves and the Hammer machines. The last 2 reps should feel hard.

Weeks 5–8: Strength and size. 4 sets of 6–10, heavier weight. The curl move changes.

Weeks 9–12: Peak. One push lift each day, 4 sets of 6–8. The curl move changes again.

Week 13: Easy week, half the sets. On Friday, test your push lift. Then weigh in and see how far you came.

Food — Keep It Simple

  • Eat protein at every meal. Eggs, meat, Greek yogurt.
  • Stop eating 2–3 hours before bed. Late-night food is the leak.
  • Half your plate is veggies.
  • Drink water before coffee in the morning.
  • Don't skip meals on hard work days. Under-eating stalls you too.

The Truth

The gym builds the arms and shoulders. The food drops the 22 lbs that lets you see them. You need both. Three short workouts you actually do beats five you skip.

Cardio guide
  • Fat loss and health: 150–300 min a week at a moderate pace (a brisk walk, about 3.5 mph), or 75–150 min of hard cardio.
  • Keep it easy on lifting days so your legs can recover.
  • A 30 min walk at 3.5 mph burns about 150 cal for a 210 lb man (estimate).

Beef Recipes

Safe Cooking

  • Ground beef: cook to 160 F.
  • Steaks, roasts and cubed meat: cook to at least 145 F, then rest 3 minutes.
  • Crockpot: thaw the meat all the way first. Never start from frozen.
  • Recipe times do not count thawing. Thaw meat in the fridge.
